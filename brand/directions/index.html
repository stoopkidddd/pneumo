Why this one. Why not.

The most literal connection to pneumatic mail, with sturdy condensed lettering and equipment-red controls. It feels purposeful before it feels decorative. The motion bars are supporting detail, not the identity; at small sizes the carrier’s ribs do the work. More industrial than cozy, and the red must remain a brand action color, not an error signal.

Air Mail

A pocket-sized dispatch stamp makes every hand-off feel personal.

The bold scalloped squircle is a compact dispatch-stamp impression. Its generous negative-space channel begins as a rounded pneumatic capsule and finishes in a rightward delivery cut, explaining a tube-carried hand-off while remaining obvious at 16px.

What feels like your Pneumo?

Pick one, or mix (for example, A’s mark with B’s palette).

These are identity directions, not final exports. The next round resolves the chosen mark, adaptive icon layers, notification silhouette and production tokens.

All three keep the same promise: a little capsule, a clear destination, your devices. No lungs, crosses or clinical teal. The owner’s choice is deliberately not recorded here.
